About

A student community for practical cybersecurity

CSSC UAJK is a student-led club at the University of Azad Jammu & Kashmir in Muzaffarabad. We focus on learning by doing: exploring real concepts, practicing in safe environments, and building skills step by step with peers who are curious and committed.

Our mission is to help students learn, practice, compete, and share knowledge responsibly. We encourage ethical and legal use of cybersecurity skills at all times, and we only support learning activities done with proper permission and respect for others.

What We Do

Activities that build real skills

Workshops

Guided sessions that introduce core cybersecurity topics in a practical, beginner-friendly way.

CTFs & Hackathons

Team-based challenges that sharpen problem-solving, collaboration, and applied security thinking.

Hands-on Labs

Practice-focused activities that help members learn by doing in controlled, ethical environments.

Security Awareness

Sessions that help students recognize risks, follow safer practices, and think critically online.
